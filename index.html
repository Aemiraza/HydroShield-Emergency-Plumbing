Emergency: How To Shut Off Water Fast

Follow these 4 steps immediately to prevent thousands in flood damage while our plumber is in route.

1

Locate Main Shut-Off Valve

Check your front flower bed near the street curb, utility room, or garage wall. Turn the brass lever perpendicular (90°) or rotate the round handle clockwise until tight.

2

Protect Your Water Heater

Turn off the circuit breaker labeled 'Water Heater' on your electrical panel. If it's a gas heater, twist the thermostat dial to 'PILOT' or 'OFF' so the heating elements don't burn out dry.

3

Depressurize Your Plumbing System

Open the lowest outdoor hose bib / garden spigot outside your home. This drains the remaining water in the pipes safely outside rather than into your living room ceilings.

4

Call HydroShield Emergency Dispatch

Our mobile master plumber unit is standing by to take over and perform emergency repair.
